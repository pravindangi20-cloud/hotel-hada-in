Your comfortable stay in Udaipur

Tucked into the quieter lanes of Shyam Nagar in Bhuwana, opposite Opera Garden, Hotel Hada Inn is meant to be an easy, unfussy base for people visiting Udaipur — whether you're here for a wedding, a work trip, or a few days of sightseeing around the lakes and palaces.

Guests have rated the property 5.0 out of 5 across 51 Google reviews. The team's focus stays on the basics done well: a clean room, a helpful front desk, and a location that keeps you close to Chitrakoot Nagar and the rest of the city without the noise of the busier tourist strip.
